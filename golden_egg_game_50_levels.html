How to play

Find one golden egg in every row, column, and color region. Golden eggs cannot touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Single-tap an egg to put a big X directly over it. Double-tap an egg to attempt a golden egg. Tap an X once to clear it. A correct golden egg turns completely gold and stays locked in place for that level. The pale square keeps its region color throughout, so you can always track each color region.

You place your own X marks—there are no automatic X eliminations. Use Hint when you want help identifying a solution position. Early levels teach with one fixed golden egg and a separate single-square color-region clue; later levels rely more on deduction. Controls and instructions are below the full-width board. Finish a level to unlock the next.
