How to play

Find one golden egg in every row, column, and color region. Golden eggs cannot touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Single-tap an egg to put a big X directly over it. Tap that X again to place a golden egg; tap a golden egg again to clear it. The pale square keeps its region color throughout, so you can always track each color region. You do not need a separate mode or gold button.

Auto X marks positions ruled out by confirmed golden eggs. Hints point to a solution position. Finish a level to unlock the next.

Level 1: Find the forced starting egg, then use rows, columns, regions, and diagonals to deduce the rest.

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. Pale squares retain their region colors; A single tap puts a large X directly over the egg; the pale region-colored square stays unchanged. Puzzle uniqueness and difficulty still need solver-based verification.
