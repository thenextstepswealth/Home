The central idea

Don't build a nest egg.
Build a Golden Goose.

A Golden Goose is productive capital designed to keep working for you.

Build it. Grow it. Shift your strategy.

Steps 3–5 form the core wealth-building progression: build productive capital, optimize how it compounds, then shift your strategy as the portfolio becomes capable of doing more of the work.

  • BUILD — create substantial productive capital
  • GROW — improve how efficiently it compounds
  • SHIFT — let compounding do more of the work
  • THRIVE — use wealth to support the life you want

Why it matters

The goal is not simply to accumulate a larger balance. It is to build an asset base that can increasingly produce growth and income without requiring the same level of ongoing contribution.
