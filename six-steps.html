THE SIX-STEP WEALTH-BUILDING JOURNEY

Six Steps. One Journey.

Explore each step of the journey and see how the pieces fit together.

STEP 1 · FREEDOM

BREAK FREE

Build your financial foundation.

Eliminate debt, create positive cash flow, and get your financial house in order so you can start building wealth.

This step establishes the financial foundation. The framework emphasizes building before accelerating wealth building, with attention to consumer debt, emergency funds, budgeting, cash flow, financial stability, and financial confidence.

STEP 2 · SECURE

SECURE YOUR FUTURE

Build a safety net retirement that lets you take bold steps towards growing your future wealth in the next step.

Calculate what you're likely to need in retirement, determine how much you need to contribute, and evaluate whether additional retirement contributions remain the best use of your next dollar as you pursue your broader wealth-building goals.

The focus is retirement security within the broader journey. The Next Dollar framework asks where you are, what you are trying to accomplish, what priorities are competing for the next dollar, and what potential benefits, risks, and trade-offs should be considered.

STEP 3 · BUILD

DON’T BUILD A NEST EGG. BUILD A GOLDEN GOOSE.

Build a substantial pool of productive capital.

Once your foundation and retirement are on track, consider directing available next dollars toward building an investment portfolio designed to harness the power of compounding.

This is the Golden Goose. A Golden Goose is a pool of productive capital designed to harness the power of compounding and potentially produce future wealth and/or income. Productive capital is capital positioned with the objective of generating future growth and/or income.

STEP 4 · GROW

GIVE YOUR GOOSE WINGS

Optimize your Golden Goose for long-term growth.

Learn how to evaluate investments, historical performance, risk, fees, diversification, taxes, and compounding so you can make informed decisions about the factors that can influence how your wealth grows.

The emphasis is understanding before acting. Approved investment education topics include historical performance, risk, volatility, fees, diversification, taxes, compounding, time horizon, investment objectives, investment characteristics, evaluating investments, comparing characteristics, and understanding trade-offs.

STEP 5 · SHIFT

TEACH YOUR GOOSE TO SOAR

Learn when to shift your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

Once your Golden Goose is on track to produce the Golden Eggs you'll need by the time you're ready to use them, learn how and when to consider shifting your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

This reflects the journey principle that priorities can change as circumstances change. The framework is not a rigid formula; individuals may revisit, address, or evaluate different steps based on their circumstances, goals, and priorities.

STEP 6 · THRIVE

LIVE YOUR BEST LIFE

Enjoy your wealth and build a lasting legacy.

Use the income from your Golden Goose alongside your earned income to live the life you want today, while continuing to strengthen your retirement strategy and build a legacy for generations to come.

Wealth is ultimately intentional. The framework defines Golden Eggs as the future income, benefits, or financial resources generated by the Golden Goose, and emphasizes that wealth should ultimately serve the life, goals, values, and legacy a person wants to create.
