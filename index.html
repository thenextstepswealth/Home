The central idea

Don't build a nest egg. Build a Golden Goose.

A nest egg is money you accumulate. A Golden Goose is productive capital designed to keep working for you.

The goal of Steps 3–5 is to build it, optimize it and eventually let it carry more of the load — so you can shift from constantly contributing toward wealth to increasingly benefiting from the wealth you've built.

BUILDCreate substantial productive capital.
GROWImprove how efficiently it compounds.
SHIFTLet compounding do more of the work.
THRIVEUse wealth to support the life you want.

A practical approach to wealth building

Wealth building should have a roadmap.

The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey was created to give people a clear progression beyond simply getting out of debt or saving for retirement.

It is a framework for thinking about the financial decisions that come after the basics — building productive capital, optimizing it, shifting your strategy as your wealth grows, and ultimately using that wealth to live well and create a lasting legacy.

✓Education focused and designed to build financial confidence.
✓Built around your current position rather than a one-size-fits-all checklist.
