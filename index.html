Wealth building is a journey, not a single destination.

The goal isn’t simply to accumulate money. It’s to build a financial foundation, make intentional decisions about your money, grow productive capital, and ultimately create the freedom to live the life you’ve envisioned.

THE SIX-STEP WEALTH-BUILDING JOURNEY

Six Steps. One Journey.

The Six-Step Wealth-Building Journey provides a progression and common framework—not a rigid one-size-fits-all formula—and serves as the central framework across Next Steps offerings.

STEP 1 · FREEDOM

BREAK FREE

Build your financial foundation.

Eliminate debt, create positive cash flow, and get your financial house in order so you can start building wealth.

STEP 2 · SECURE

SECURE YOUR FUTURE

Build a safety net retirement that lets you take bold steps towards growing your future wealth in the next step.

Calculate what you’re likely to need in retirement, determine how much you need to contribute, and avoid overfunding retirement if it can be better used to accelerate overall wealth-building.

STEP 3 · BUILD

DON’T BUILD A NEST EGG. BUILD A GOLDEN GOOSE.

Build a substantial pool of productive capital.

Once your foundation and retirement are on track, consider directing available next dollars toward building an investment portfolio designed to harness the power of compounding.

STEP 4 · GROW

GIVE YOUR GOOSE WINGS

Optimize your Golden Goose for long-term growth.

Learn how to evaluate investments, historical performance, risk, fees, diversification, taxes, and compounding so you can make informed decisions about how to potentially increase the rate at which your wealth grows.

STEP 5 · SHIFT

TEACH YOUR GOOSE TO SOAR

Learn when to shift your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

Once your Golden Goose is on track to produce the Golden Eggs you’ll need by the time you’re ready to use them, learn how and when to consider shifting your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

STEP 6 · THRIVE

LIVE YOUR BEST LIFE

Enjoy your wealth and build a lasting legacy.

Use the income from your Golden Goose alongside your earned income to live the life you want today, while continuing to strengthen your retirement strategy and build a legacy for generations to come.

THE CHALLENGE

Know where you are. Know what comes next.

The hardest part of wealth building is often knowing what to do next. The Next Steps journey helps you identify where you are today, focus on the challenge in front of you, and take the actions that can move you forward.

Identify

Understand where you are today, what you have accomplished, and what deserves your attention next.

Focus

Concentrate your time, money, and decisions on the priorities that matter most at your current step.

Advance

Take purposeful action, build momentum, and progress through the wealth-building journey one step at a time.
