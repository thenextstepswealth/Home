The Six Levels

Find your place on the journey.

Each graphic below comes directly from your Wealth-Building Journey graphic.

1
FREEDOM

Break Free

Build your financial foundation.

Eliminate debt, create positive cash flow, and get your financial house in order so wealth building can begin.

Open Step 1 →
2
SECURE

Secure Your Future

Build a safety net for retirement.

Calculate what you're likely to need in retirement, determine how much you need to contribute, and avoid overfunding retirement if it can be better used to accelerate overall wealth-building.

Open Step 2 →
3
BUILD

Build Your Golden Goose

Build a substantial pool of productive capital.

Once your foundation and retirement are on track, focus your available next dollars on building an investment portfolio designed to harness the power of compounding.

Open Step 3 →
4
GROW

Give Your Goose Wings

Optimize your Golden Goose for long-term growth.

Learn how to evaluate investments, historical performance, risk, fees, diversification, taxes, and compounding so you can make informed decisions about how to potentially increase the rate at which your wealth grows.

Open Step 4 →
5
SHIFT

Teach Your Goose to Soar

Shift your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

Once your Golden Goose is on track to reach your target by your chosen retirement age, reduce what you need to contribute and redirect more income toward retirement, tax-advantaged accounts, and other wealth-building opportunities.

Open Step 5 →
6
THRIVE

Live Your Best Life

Enjoy your wealth and build a lasting legacy.

Use the income from your Golden Goose alongside your earned income to live the life you want today, while continuing to maximize retirement contributions and build a legacy for generations to come.

Open Step 6 →

Don't build a nest egg. Build a Golden Goose.
