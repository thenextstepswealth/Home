STEP 5

TEACH YOUR GOOSE TO SOAR

THEME: SHIFT

Shift your strategy and let compounding do more of the work.

Detailed Explanation

Once your Golden Goose is on track to reach your target by your chosen retirement age, reduce what you need to contribute and redirect more of your income toward retirement, tax-advantaged accounts, and other wealth-building opportunities.

Key Actions

  • Right-size contributions to your Golden Goose
  • Redirect more income to retirement and tax-advantaged accounts
  • Take advantage of new opportunities
  • Keep your Goose on track with periodic reviews
Core Principle:
Shift your strategy as your wealth grows and let compounding do more of the work.
