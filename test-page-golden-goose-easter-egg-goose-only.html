THE NEXT STEPS APPROACH

Wealth Is More Than a Destination

Building wealth isn't just about someday reaching a financial destination. It's about building for your future while finding ways to experience the benefits of that wealth along the journey.

In the Next Steps approach, we use the metaphor of a Golden Goose: build productive capital today, allow it to grow, and over time it may produce Golden Eggs—income and other financial resources you can use along the way while continuing to build for the future.

Build for tomorrow. Live the journey today.

THE CHALLENGE

Start Where You Are

Your next step depends on where you are today.

Before deciding what to do with your money, it helps to understand your current financial position, what you're trying to accomplish, and what priorities are competing for your next dollar.

The Next Steps approach starts with understanding—not with a predetermined financial solution.

The Next Dollar

At every stage of your journey, you may have competing priorities for your available money. The Next Dollar Framework helps you consider where that next dollar may have the greatest potential impact based on your goals and circumstances.

Understand Before You Act

Financial decisions involve potential benefits, risks, trade-offs, and consequences. The goal isn't simply to take action—it's to understand your options well enough to make informed decisions.
