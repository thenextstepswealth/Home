ABOUT THE NEXT STEPS

The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey

Wealth building is a journey rather than a single financial decision.

The framework helps you understand where you are, identify what matters most, determine your next step, take action, reassess as circumstances change, and continue progressing.

The six steps provide a framework—not a rigid one-size-fits-all formula. The right focus depends on your circumstances and broader wealth-building goals.

What Next Steps Is

A framework for understanding where you are and what comes next in your wealth-building journey.

Who It Helps

People who want greater clarity about their current financial position and the actions and priorities that matter most for their situation.

How Coaching Works

Education, guidance, questions, calculations, frameworks, and accountability while you remain the decision-maker.
