About The Next Steps

A roadmap beyond the basics.

The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey is a six-level framework designed to help people think about what comes after the basic financial foundations.

From financial freedom to a lasting legacy.

The framework progresses from getting your financial house in order, to securing retirement, building productive capital, optimizing growth, shifting strategy as wealth grows, and ultimately using wealth to support the life you want.

It is designed to answer two questions: Where am I today? and What should I focus on next?

The Six Levels

1. Freedom — Break Free

2. Secure — Secure Your Future

3. Build — Build Your Golden Goose

4. Grow — Give Your Goose Wings

5. Shift — Teach Your Goose to Soar

6. Thrive — Live Your Best Life
