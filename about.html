The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey

A framework for turning wealth building into a clear journey with a next step you can understand and act on.

Why the journey matters

Wealth building is not one decision or one account. It is a progression. The Next Steps framework is designed to help you understand the stage you are in, focus on the challenge in front of you, and build toward the next stage.

The philosophy

Start with the foundation. Protect what matters. Build productive capital. Optimize it. Let compounding do more of the work. Then use the wealth you have built to live your best life.
