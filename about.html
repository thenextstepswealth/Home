About The Next Steps

A roadmap for what comes next.

The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey was created to provide a clear progression beyond simply getting out of debt or saving for retirement.

It is a framework for understanding where you are, what deserves attention now, and what needs to happen to reach the next level of wealth building.

One step at a time.

The goal isn’t to solve everything at once. It is to identify the right challenge, focus your resources, take action, and keep progressing.
