About The Next Steps

A practical framework for moving from financial stability toward wealth and legacy.

A Roadmap for What Comes Next

The Next Steps Wealth-Building Journey is designed to give people a clear progression after they have established the basics of personal finance. Instead of treating wealth building as one giant goal, the journey breaks it into six understandable stages.

Each stage builds on the one before it, creating a framework that can be applied to different starting points, goals, and life circumstances.
