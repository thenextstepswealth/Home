YOUR BOXER

Your real photo appears over the boxer’s head during the fight.
How to set your photo: 1) Tap Take picture or Upload photo. 2) Choose/capture the image and wait for “Photo ready.” 3) Check that the preview appears. 4) Tap Save boxer. 5) Start/restart the fight. The face is shown inside the boxer sprite. Photos stay in this browser; they are not uploaded to a server.

OPPONENT

Give the opponent a real face too, or keep the default.
Opponent photo steps: 1) Tap Take picture or Upload photo. 2) Wait for “Photo ready” and check the preview. 3) Tap Save opponent. The opponent photo is then used in the fight.
