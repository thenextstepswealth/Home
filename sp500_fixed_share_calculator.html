Select a starting year. The first year's withdrawal determines a fixed number of hypothetical S&P 500 shares; that same number is withdrawn every January. The dollar withdrawal changes with the January index level.

Model mechanics
  • The first withdrawal is the chosen percentage of the initial portfolio. Fixed shares = first withdrawal ÷ January S&P 500 index level in the starting year.
  • Each later January withdrawal = fixed shares × that year's January S&P 500 index level.
  • For a simplified annual balance calculation, the dollar withdrawal is subtracted at the beginning of the year, then the remaining balance receives that year's total return, including dividends.
  • If the remaining balance cannot fund the full scheduled withdrawal, the calculator records the amount actually available and marks the portfolio depleted.
Data and limitations: January prices are monthly-average index levels, not the exact first trading day's close. Price series is available for 1926–2025. Annual total-return inputs include dividends and are rounded; early historical returns are reconstructed estimates. This is an educational approximation, not a daily/monthly fund-level backtest. Taxes, fees, inflation, and the difference between index price returns and total-return reinvestment mechanics are not separately modeled.

Price source: S&P 500 Historical Prices. Total-return methodology/reference: Damodaran historical returns (includes dividends) and Shiller monthly total-return dataset information.
