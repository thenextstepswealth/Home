Retirement safety-net calculation: The calculator estimates the amount needed at retirement by calculating the present value of the projected retirement-income shortfall over the planning horizon, using the selected return during retirement and inflation assumptions.

Planning horizon: The calculator uses age 102 as the fixed planning age. Retirement-income payments are calculated from the retirement age through age 102.

Important: This tool is for education and planning discussion. It is not investment, tax, legal or individualized financial advice.
AARP-method benchmark: The retirement-income and savings calculations follow the structure shown in the AARP Retirement Nest Egg Calculator results. AARP describes Social Security as a sliding-scale estimate based on income, years worked and retirement age; this calculator uses a simplified planning proxy and does not reproduce an individual's official Social Security benefit.
Calculation note: Pre-retirement savings are projected annually using the selected return and contribution assumptions. Contributions are made through the year before retirement; the retirement year itself does not include a contribution. Retirement income is projected annually for the fixed age-102 planning horizon, with inflation applied to both retirement income and estimated Social Security. Social Security is shown as an editable planning estimate. Users can replace the auto-populated estimate with the annual amount shown in their SSA account.
